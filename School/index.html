Nurturing Minds, Building Futures

Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

What classes are offered at Shree New Best Navkar School?

We offer comprehensive education from Pre-School (KG, LKG, UKG) up to Middle School (Std. 1 to Std. 9) in English Medium.

What documents are required for admission?

Required documents include Child's Birth Certificate, Transfer Certificate (for Std. 2 to 9), Passport-size photographs, and Aadhaar card copies.

Is transport facility available for nearby areas?

Yes! Safe school buses and vans cover Chuda, Bhesan, Galath Chokdi, and neighboring villages with dedicated drivers and attendants.
